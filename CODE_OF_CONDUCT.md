# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive
feedback, and focus disagreements on ideas and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This concise policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
